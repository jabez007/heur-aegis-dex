## Summary
Implement a detailed Pokémon roster expansion and Radar charts for stat visualization.

## Environment
- **Product/Service**: Heur-Aegis Dex
- **Region/Version**: v1.0.0

## Expected Behavior
1. **Typing Roster**: Clicking a `PokemonCard` header should expand a list showing all Pokémon that qualify for that specific type combination, rather than only allowing one-by-one cycling.
2. **Radar Charts**: In the Pokémon Summary (Stats) view, provide an option to switch between the current Bar Charts and a "Radar/Spider" chart to visualize stat distribution (ATK/DEF/HP).

## Impact
**Low** - Cosmetic and informational enhancement to improve the "Dex" feel of the application.

## Additional Context
The Radar chart was a key visual feature of the original PoGo-PvP-Rankings app.
