## Summary
Add a global search bar in the header for instant Pokémon lookup and quick-selection.

## Environment
- **Product/Service**: Heur-Aegis Dex
- **Region/Version**: v1.0.0

## Expected Behavior
1. **Global Search**: A text input in the `GbaHeader` component.
2. **Jump to Type**: Searching for a specific Pokémon (e.g., "Garchomp") should automatically update the Cup Filters to include its types (Dragon/Ground) and scroll the Meta Analysis grid to that specific card.
3. **Quick Select**: Add a button in the search results to instantly add the searched Pokémon to the Team Workbench.

## Impact
**High** - Significantly improves UX by allowing users to build teams around specific favorites without manual filtering.
