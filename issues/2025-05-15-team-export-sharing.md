## Summary
Implement "Export to Clipboard" for sharing team configurations.

## Environment
- **Product/Service**: Heur-Aegis Dex
- **Region/Version**: v1.0.0

## Expected Behavior
1. **Export Button**: Add an "Export Team" button to the `TeamWorkbench` actions.
2. **Clipboard Format**: Clicking the button should generate a formatted Markdown or text block containing:
   - Team Member Names
   - Typings
   - Key Stats (Total, Average)
   - Identified Team Weaknesses
3. **Confirmation**: Show a "Team Copied!" GBA notification upon success.

## Impact
**Low** - Social and utility feature for community sharing.
